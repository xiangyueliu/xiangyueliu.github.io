About

I'm a fourth-year PhD student at Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (expected graduation in June 2027), advised by Prof. Ping Tan.

I was fortunate to intern at Tencent Hunyuan Foundation Model Team, where I contributed to Hy Image Pre-Training, e.g. model architecture designs of Hy Image 3.5. Besides, I also worked on next architecture of Hy Image series.

My research works toward multimodal foundation models and pretraining, including:

  • Multimodal Foundation Model Architecture Hy Image 3.5, Symbiotic-MoE (ECCV 2026), Rosetta (NeurIPS 2026)
  • Image Generation NoiseAR, Plan-and-Paint
  • 3D Generation & Editing GenN2N (CVPR 2024), GaussianAvatar-Editor (3DV 2025)
